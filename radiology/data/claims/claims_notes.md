## The Hinton arc

- **2016-10-27, CDL "Machine Learning and the Market for Intelligence", Rotman/U of T (C02).** He said "People should stop training radiologists now." He predicted deep learning would beat radiologists "within 5 years" because it would "get a lot more experience", hedged to "might be 10 years", and opened with the Wile E. Coyote line. He also said he'd already said this at a hospital and "it didn't go down too well." Video: "Geoff Hinton: On Radiology" (Creative Destruction Lab, youtube.com/watch?v=2HMPRXstSvQ, uploaded 2016-11-24). **Verified** from the transcript in david-brain (`vault/web-captures/web-capture-2026-10-11-geoff-hinton-on-radiology.md`); timestamps were stripped, but these are his opening lines.
- **2017-04-03, New Yorker / Mukherjee (C04).** He repeated "stop training radiologists now" in print.
- **2023-10-08, 60 Minutes (C12).** He said AI is "already comparable with radiologists" at reading images. That is a claim of parity, not replacement.
- **2023-12-08, Topol's Ground Truths (C13). This is the real first walkback.** He called 2016 "a daring and incorrect prediction" and said he "meant it for interpreting medical scans, not for doing everything a radiologist does". He added "I was off by about a factor of three, but I'm still convinced I was completely right in the long term." He now expects AI as a second opinion in about 10 years and as the first opinion in about 15.
- **2025-05-14, NYT / Lohr (C15).** The widely cited concession: he "spoke too broadly" and was wrong on timing, not direction. AI plus radiologist "will make radiologists a whole lot more efficient." The NYT was blocked for our fetcher, so these words come via AuntMinnie and The Decoder.

## The Huang arc

- **2018-03-27, GTC keynote, Project Clara launch (C08).** He framed AI as an assistant "sitting next to" every radiologist. This is the earliest Huang radiology framing we found, and it is augment from the start.
- **2025-11-19, U.S.-Saudi Investment Forum, with Musk (C18).** "The prediction that radiologists would be the first jobs to go was exactly the opposite", and "more radiologists are being hired now as a result of AI." Wording differs between Fortune and Radiology Business.
- **2026-03-23, Lex Fridman #494 (C20), verified in the official transcript.** "Every radiology platform and package today is driven by AI, and yet the number of radiologists grew." He also said the alarmist warning "scared people from doing this profession" and that there is now a radiologist shortage.
- **2026-09-23, Ezra Klein Show (C22).** Here he names Hinton: "it's irresponsible to say all that" and "All of his predictions have been wrong." Context: the "irresponsible" line answered Hinton's 10% doom estimate, and Huang then used radiology as the test case.
- **We found no evidence that Huang ever predicted radiologists would be replaced.** His story has a consistent shape: "they said radiology would go first; the opposite happened." The fact it rests on, radiologist headcount growing, is what makes it worth checking against the data.

## Misattributed or misdated quotes (worth a beat)

1. **Hinton's remark is usually dated November 2016. The event was 2016-10-27.** CDL's own blog (posted 2016-11-03) dates the conference to October 27. The November date almost certainly comes from the video, which Creative Destruction Lab uploaded on 2016-11-24.
2. **"Huang: radiology is the first job AI will destroy... I advise nobody goes into this field" (C23).** A Webull AU item (Aug 2026, citing a June "Sequoia" conversation) presents this as Huang's own prediction. In every verified talk Huang is *recounting* what "computer scientists, AI researchers" said. Treat it as misattributed until someone hears the recording.
3. **"Radiologists who use AI will replace radiologists who don't."** This was Curtis Langlotz at RSNA in November 2017, in print in May 2019. It is often credited to "a Stanford study" or to other people.
4. **Andrew Ng never said "radiologists should be worried."** On 2017-11-15 he *asked* "Should radiologists be worried about their jobs?" and then claimed CheXNet beat radiologists, measured against 4 of them. In May 2021 he described the "proof-of-concept-to-production gap" (C11).
5. **Khosla's 2019 line has two versions:** "plans to practice in 10 years" (TechCrunch) and "continues to practice" (AuntMinnie). His deadline also moved, from obsolete by 2022 (said in 2017) to 2029 (said in 2019).
6. **Hinton's NYT 2025 concession is treated as his first walkback, but he had already said "I was wrong" in December 2023 (Topol).**
7. **"AI isn't replacing radiologists" is a blog headline about Deena Mousa's piece.** The actual Works in Progress title is "The algorithm will see you now" (2025-09-25).
