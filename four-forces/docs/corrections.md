# Corrections: what we got wrong or glossed in the Four Forces Reel

Found in the fact-check pass on 2026-10-16, after the Reel shipped. They got caught up in the video production process. None of them changes the story, but we'd rather call ourselves out than leave them in the fine print. This file is written to go into the public repo's project README as-is.

1. **"Loan processing" also removed two insurance jobs.** The waterfall's third step removed four occupations: loan officers, loan interviewers and clerks, insurance claims and policy processing clerks, and insurance underwriters. The script's reason, higher interest rates, fits the two loan jobs but not insurance claims. Removing only the loan jobs leaves about **877,000** instead of the **870,000** shown (and the loan bar becomes about −250,000 instead of −260,000).

2. **"Other jobs" meant two different things.** On the waterfall, "Gap vs. other jobs" compares the most AI-exposed occupations with all occupations BLS doesn't rate "very high" (571 occupations, balanced panel). On the paralegal chart, "Other jobs +6.6%" is all jobs outside office and administrative support (BLS official totals). Both are fair benchmarks, but they're different groups and shouldn't share a label.

3. **"The ChatGPT era, between 2022 and 2025" starts six months early.** BLS's occupation survey is taken in May, so the window is May 2022 to May 2025. ChatGPT launched November 30, 2022.

4. **Paralegals aren't the same "office work" as the fast lane.** BLS files paralegals under legal occupations, not office and administrative support. "That's office work too" works in conversation, but it isn't the same category as the fast lane's office and admin jobs.

Everything else in the script checked out against the data. See `docs/numbers-by-period.md` for every figure with its timeframe and source.
